# Cello 2.1 Cell-Specific Pathways

Cell-specific pathway maps appear only when the matching cell model is active.

The maps are assembled exclusively from processes already represented by Cello's model outputs. They are explanatory pathway views and not comprehensive biochemical reaction networks.

The pancreatic beta-cell pathway is a glucose-stimulated insulin-secretion map; proinsulin biosynthesis and processing are not represented as a fully resolved kinetic pathway in v2.1.
